PHISHING SECURITY ANALYSIS REPORT
Date: March 14, 2026
Subject: Analysis of Retirement-Themed Phishing Campaign
Classification: Internal / Security Research
1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
This report analyzes a targeted phishing campaign aimed at individuals nearing retirement. The attack leverages high-pressure social engineering to harvest sensitive financial identifiers, specifically targeting Bank Verification Numbers (BVN) and account credentials.
2. ATTACK SCENARIO
 * Vector: Email-based Social Engineering.
 * Context: Impersonation of a pension or financial institution.
 * Trigger: Claims that retirement funds are ready for disbursement but require "immediate verification" to prevent account suspension.
 * Goal: Unauthorized access to retirement accounts and identity theft.
3. INDICATORS OF COMPROMISE (IoCs)
 * Data Request: Explicit solicitation of PII (BVN and full account details).
 * Psychological Trigger: Exploitation of the "Retirement Milestone" to bypass typical skepticism.
 * Source Discrepancy: Likely lack of valid SPF/DKIM/DMARC records on the sender's domain.
4. VULNERABILITY ASSESSMENT
| Category | Identified Gap |
|---|---|
| Technical | Lack of DMARC Enforcement, allowing domain spoofing. |
| Authentication | Absence of Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) on the target accounts. |
| Human Factor | Gaps in Security Awareness Training regarding official banking communication protocols. |
5. MITIGATION STRATEGIES
 * Enforce Strict DMARC Policies: Prevent unauthorized senders from using the organization's domain.
 * MFA Implementation: Deploy hardware tokens or app-based MFA for all financial portals.
 * Communication Protocol: Establish that BVN and sensitive data are never requested via email.
